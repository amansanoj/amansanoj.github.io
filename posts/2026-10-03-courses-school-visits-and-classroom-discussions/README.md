# Courses, School Visits, and Classroom Discussions

A few updates from outside my project repositories, collected in one place.

## Back at School

In July, I returned to GEMS Our Own Indian School with AbdulRahman Maniar and Theyjashri K Saravanan to judge its Project-Based Learning showcase. We had just graduated, so it was an unusual change to be evaluating presentations in a place where we’d recently been presenting our own work.

It was also a chance to catch up with Prathima Nag, who had made time for our technology projects while we were students.

In October, I led a discussion on the software development life cycle during a flipped classroom session with Zeenath Reza Khan. Both events involved explaining and discussing technical work with other people, rather than working through it alone.

## Coursework

In March 2025, I completed an eight-week Data Science and AI course from IIT Madras through its Centre for Outreach and Digital Education.

This year, I also completed the AI and Penetration Testing track of Khalifa University’s AI and Cyber Security summer school initiative, along with Cisco’s Computer Hardware Basics and Operating Systems Basics courses. The Cisco labs covered component identification, Windows administrative tools, and Linux filesystem and log navigation.

The One Million Prompters initiative covered generative AI and prompt construction. In the Mastercard cybersecurity virtual experience on Forage, I worked through a simulated phishing-awareness exercise: revising a sample email, reviewing the supplied engagement data, and preparing training for the departments with higher failure rates.

That was a simulation, not employment at Mastercard or a live test against its workforce.

These are course completions. I’ll use separate project posts for work that shows how I applied the material, rather than treating a credential as evidence of production experience.
